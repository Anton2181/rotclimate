In the candidate's actual states, the cap binds35.2% of scored cell-steps in
Volga and reduces its June/July positive target cooling by about4.74/4.73 K.
Those are actual candidate-stage changes, not frozen-control causal cuts.
The saved figure shows improved western summer temperatures, persistent
spring/winter problems, and excessive summer warming in Ungava. Rainfall
tradeoffs also persist. British Columbia's T MAE worsens0.251 C: being cold
in annual mean does not imply every season needs warming.

This is useful evidence that wetness cooling can compensate for other thermal
errors. It does not identify the assumed heat capacity as correct or justify
a regional switch chosen from observed errors. A single shared solar cap does
not resolve the cold/warm continental split. The next thermal formulation
must address upstream/seasonal state and actual heat-column assumptions,
rather than tune the bound after this failure.
