exterior thermal-pressure context to the original child yields **779.19**.
The Algerian parent rains.54 mm/year, while its nested child rains360.48.

missing pressure context alone. Child moisture boundaries and other
domain-dependent processes matter; a complete parent is not pressure truth.

belts. A symmetric equinox test gives7 m/s at45N but0 at45S, and−3 at75N but0
at75S. Eleven symmetry/known-answer tests fail and the rotation check passes

parent, which extends into southern midlatitudes. The fixed pilot was completed
with its original source; none of its runs was changed or retried. A separate
mechanical source correction and checks are next, not a rescue of this study.
