## A. Calibration (method)
A1 **Map-edge test** (1-2 h compute). Region cases are 2,500 km maps (pad 1,100 km); the atlas runs a whole world.
   Somalia showed a gate (e55b) that cannot fire because the driving heat low is off the map, and edge humidity
   is a fixed boundary. Rerun r52 at pad 2,200 km on ~12 regions (Somalia, Congo, north India, Sahara, Bengal,
   prairies, Chile, Japan, ...). If scores move by more than noise, the fit is absorbing small-map artefacts that the
   atlas does not have: then calibrate on larger maps (or a coarse whole-Earth run, A4) for edge-sensitive knobs.
A2 **Regime-stratified scoring** (cheap). Tag regions into ~6 regimes (tropical wet, tropical dry/monsoon, hot
   desert, Mediterranean, cold continental, maritime/mountain) and report S per regime alongside S61. Targeted
   mechanisms show signal inside their regime without being drowned by 55 unaffected regions.
A3 **Policy v6 proposal (owner decision).** Add the spent S18 regions to the dev pool (79 regions; includes Somalia,
   Congo) and add a guard "no regime worse than +2 %" to the v5 adoption rule. Sealed U18 stays single-use.
A4 DROPPED after A1: the atlas is a padded 3,680 x 1,703 km map, not a whole world. **Whole-Earth run feasibility** (check only). A coarse global Earth run is the deployment configuration of the
   atlas model; if it runs in reasonable time it is the honest test for circulation-driven errors.
A5 **No more global PPE of old knobs.** PPE only as a small follow-up after a new mechanism (its knobs + 4-5
   rebalancing knobs), as W3 did. Several adopted mechanisms are bundled into ONE r53 finalist so U18 is spent once.

## B. Model structure (ordered by error budget)
B1 **Continental winter too mild / range too small** (largest T term). Diagnose with land_budget on prairies,
   Midwest, Manchuria, Tibet: which stage keeps January warm. Candidate: a snow-covered, calm winter surface
   decouples from the mixed air (surface inversion) so eddy heat resupply is weaker over snow at low wind.
B2 **Mediterranean / monsoon range too large.** Diagnose whether summer is too hot or winter too cold (Levant,
   Anatolia, S. Italy; Bengal pre-monsoon). Candidate: sea moderation reach in enclosed seas; pre-monsoon cloud.
B3 **Rain seasonality in the mid-latitudes** (16 regions). Split simulated rain into storm-track and convective
   parts per season for Texas, Japan, Alps, BC, Cape; find which part has the wrong season.
B4 **Tropical rain trigger.** Wet interiors too dry (Congo, Amazon) while divergent dry coasts are wet (Somalia,
   Tanzania / Zambia dry season): rain responds to humidity, not to low-level convergence or stability. Candidate:
   strengthen the convergence dependence of convective rain (the conv field exists; divergence_drying is only 0.054).
B5 **Hot-dry cold bias (C1).** Dry heating helped Najd / Iran / India but hurt cold-current coastal deserts
   (Namibia, Chile). Candidate: exempt land under a marine layer (cold SST upwind within a few hundred km).
