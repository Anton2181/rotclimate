# T5 travel costs vs ORBIS — not done (21 Nov 2026)

ORBIS (Scheidel & Meeks; v1 paper orbis.stanford.edu/orbis2012/ORBIS_v1paper_20120501.pdf) computes LAND travel times from fixed mode speeds set by its authors:
- ox carts 12 km/day;
- porters and heavy mules 20 km/day;
- foot 30 km/day;
- other modes up to horse relays at 250 km/day.

Terrain enters only as a coarse adjustment for "substantial grade"; the paper says plans to model speed by slope were set aside.

Calibrating our forest, relief and water travel costs against ORBIS land times would therefore fit them to another model's assumptions. That is not an Earth observation, and the owner rule is "stick to verifiable things".

ORBIS sea times are checked in the paper only qualitatively, against recorded voyage speeds. Our sailing season is already Earth-tested.

**Outcome:** T5 is dropped as unverifiable with this source. A future land test would need recorded journey durations with known routes and seasons, for example the imperial-post records collected by A. M. Ramsay (1925). The original handoff rated that low odds.
