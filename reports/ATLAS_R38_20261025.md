# Earth r38 carried into the atlas

The repository Earth-fit atlas now uses the completed r38 world refit at
downsample 8, averaging surroundings layouts 1–3. All three members pass
their observed linear, temperature and moisture checks. The simulation was
checkpointed before export; the former r37 atlas is preserved in a local
backup under `output/atlas_r38/previous_atlases/`.

| Map measure | Previous r37 | Published r38 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Seasonal zone-fit objective | 0.175624 | 0.182806 |
| Exact painted-zone agreement | 25.91% | 34.99% |
| Mediterranean target classified dry | 20.11% | 6.41% |
| Mediterranean target classified Cs | 25.78% | 74.55% |
| Cold-dry target agreement | 5.51% | 0.00% |

The broader map improvement does not solve the painted cold-dry north.
Cold-wet and tree-zone recalls also decline. These are world-map fit
results, separate from the Earth-region evidence that adopted r38 physics.
No climate parameters or frozen research plans changed in this publication.

Political v3 layers, four gap-fill variants, trade routes, per-polity people
tables and vector downloads are included in this Earth atlas. The atlas
pages are refreshed from the existing template, and the comparison hub and
Earth description now identify r38. Political plates at gap fills 0 and 1
day and a claimed/held population figure are saved under `output/polity_map/`.
The plate renderer now falls back to an installed serif font when FreeSerif
is unavailable.

Validation: all 123 referenced layer assets exist and their image files
decode; five JavaScript climate-comparison tests pass. Chromium checks pass
for climate search, biomes, farming scenarios, province overlays, deep links,
mobile layout and the conditional settlement controls. Additional browser
checks exercise all four political variants, variant persistence after
reload, trade rendering and hiding the variant selector on other layers.
`output/atlas_r38/earth_publication.json` retains the comparison, numerical
certificates, browser results and publication hashes.

The free-fit and Whittaker political re-exports are a separate follow-up.
Hosted claude.ai artifacts are not updated by this repository publication;
the current environment has no tool for modifying those artifacts.
