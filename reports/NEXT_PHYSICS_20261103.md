The complete61-region conservative-storm comparison rejects both inherited
strength families (S61 1.026560 and1.016619). F remains unopened. Missing

parameter starts do not prove a global optimum.

A later conservative-storm experiment could isolate the *pair conductance*,
whose present arithmetic mean is
`Cij = Gij (ki Mi + kj Mj)/2`, acting on specific humidity `q=W/M`.
That permits exchange into a cell with a vanishing local rate when its
neighbour's rate is large. Over terrain or storm-track edges this may matter.

A geometric alternative has `ai=sqrt(ki Mi)` and `Cij=Gij ai aj`.
Its water source is `ai G(a q)` and loss coefficient is `ai G(a)/Mi`.
With a symmetric normalized kernel it is nonnegative, pairwise conservative,
fixes uniform q, agrees with the existing operator at uniform rate/mass, and
turns off a pair when either endpoint rate is zero. This is an alternative
unresolved mixing hypothesis, not proof that weak-rate cells should block real
storms or that it improves climate. It may also suppress legitimate moisture
transport across mountains or into dry regions.

Before climate tests, check an independent explicit pair matrix, conservation,
positivity, constant-q equilibrium, uniform-rate equivalence, endpoint limits,
Picard residuals and an integrated strict toy model. A future factorial could
separate arithmetic/geometric conductance from column-water/specific-humidity
mixing (fixed hydrostatic mass scale8km), without new regional coefficients or
planetary changes. No such candidate has been selected from reserved data.
