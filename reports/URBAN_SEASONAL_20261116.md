## What the numbers mean

These are feasible **conditional occupancy scenarios**, rather than maximum
sustainable populations, new historical observations or certified town
footprints. The central and low density readings define the original resident
requests. Reducing those requests does not refit the original built-area draws
or establish the density of an occupied footprint. The preceding five-seed
sensitivity still applies: individual places and aggregate changes depend on
the chosen size draw. This stage holds seed 1 fixed.

The earlier annual supply calculation reported ration equivalents at the full
town requests. This stage changes timing, reserve protection and dispatch rules,
then returns unsupported residents to their actual origins and recomputes
production. Comparing its final totals with the annual diagnostic therefore
does not isolate a pure calendar effect. The first-pass conditional cap is also
retained, separately from the final coupled state.

## People, workers and food are counted once

The committed [declaration](../calibration/urban_diagnosis_20261116/seasonal_coupling.json)
precedes the new results. All eight map/model/density recruitment matrices first
reproduce the canonical own-hinterland and sequential wider-region withdrawals.
Their column totals reproduce the reviewed seed-1 residents, and their complete
sequential rural grids reproduce exactly. An independent full-distance and
compensated-sum reconstruction checks every sparse link.

Each matrix link partitions **people only**. A smaller town returns people in
the same recorded source proportions; no new recruitment geography is selected
to improve its result. The original finite allocator receives those demographic
shards and reserves civilians and existing 100-person institutional cohorts
inside the same town population cap. Nonmovable people and unused link allowances
remain rural. Crop area, grain, workers and rural meals are evaluated once at
each original physical cell, never copied to every demographic shard.

Remaining rural people supply a 0.4 worker fraction. The primary scenario uses
600 annual listed-field hours/worker; the declared stress uses 300, with the
same 30 reaping days at eight hours/day. Each requested-density reading retains
its exact calibrated marketed fraction: central **0.3371937035110408**, low
**0.14049021985082757**. The flat-density 300-hour case uses that flat share;
it is not the earlier annual builder's low-density/high-share sensitivity.
These shares were calibrated for the previous annual recipe; retaining their
values does not constitute a new seasonal Earth calibration.

The existing earliest eligible harvest boundary is retained. Multiple boundary
bits are alternative dates, not multiple harvests: each cell's annual edible
cereal energy enters once. Rural stores hold 365 demand-days with 5% annual
storage loss. Only origins meeting every rural food bin may market actual
overflow; hungry sources cannot export their meals. Other rural deficits remain
visible. Missing rural food imports and omitted diet/household/draft/transport
labour prevent an all-world food-feasible claim.

## Seasonal grain routes and stocks

Four separate freight matrices are reconstructed at the existing sampled travel
months 0/3/6/9. Their arithmetic mean, source owners and full-precision source
retentions match the reviewed annual freight cache **exactly**. The historical
supply-versus-placement track-order distinction is preserved. Travel physics,
town nodes, original travel-density fields and painted roads remain fixed.

Each sampled travel season supplies its three corresponding accounting bins.
This is an explicit quarterly travel approximation, not twelve newly solved
monthly networks. Each grain leg pays its existing delivered-share loss and
takes one accounting bin. Source exports have their own funded one-year stores;
intended uniform dispatch is capped by their actual repeating inventory and
route availability.

Towns protect their own current trial meals with 90-day stores first. Only
occupied towns fully supported by direct arrivals may export, and only their
actual monthly overflow. The existing cheapest-first link rule sends it toward
next-bin direct-only deficits. Importers never re-export. Final town inventories
include direct and inter-town arrivals; exported overflow is subtracted from
otherwise-unused grain exactly once. Every source, export and town stock change,
meal, spoilage, unused amount and transport loss closes in one physical ledger.

## Physical certification is distinct from a numerical fixed point

The original 64-step reference pilot did not converge and remains explicitly
**unqualified**. A common 512-step continuation also failed the strict fixed-point
criterion in a preserved raw/control case, even when its actual monthly meals
passed: the existing permanent-food ceiling includes a small downward safety
factor, so applying it repeatedly can keep shaving residents.

The preserved failures and exact model snapshots are under `reference_pilot/`.
No failed run was relabelled. The separately committed
[A3 certification stage](../calibration/urban_diagnosis_20261116/seasonal_certificate_a3.json)
uses the existing allocator's returned resident/cohort partition as a **frozen
proposal**, then recomputes physical rural people, workers, production, stores
and routes afresh at that exact population. It accepts the proposal only when
the original food, work, population, route and stock checks pass. Its parent
trial population/arrivals are retained so the actual finite allocation can be
reproduced independently. A further conservative allocator proposal is diagnostic,
not a condition for the frozen state's real monthly meals to balance.

All cases use the same method, a minimum of 64 and maximum of 512 downward
steps, and the first passing physical certificate. Numerical `converged` and
`physical_certificate` are separate fields. No global optimum is claimed and
the original physical feasibility tolerances are unchanged.

The [A1 stock implementation](../calibration/urban_diagnosis_20261116/seasonal_inventory_kernel_a1.json)
derives the fixed state of the same clipped-affine lossy annual stock map,
then reuses the canonical monthly replay. It avoids repeatedly bisecting every
physical cell at every coupled trial. Same-input canonical controls cover all
16 initial cases, 64 inventories and five manufactured patterns. Maximum
deviation is 0.583 of the original allowed inventory tolerance; support flags
are unchanged. Canonical stock/work/finite-allocation modules remain intact.
The original [A2 continuation](../calibration/urban_diagnosis_20261116/seasonal_solver_a2.json)
and its failure are also retained.

rerun. The compact `food_ceiling` field is the parent allocator's conservative

the fresh physical state. Full source/stock caches remain ignored and reproducible.

retaining original node/actor order and affiliation. Their adoption gates remain
false. The existing P2/v2.1 political rules read institutional-active source
sets, not civilian totals. A separate dependency audit either proves identity
with the published headline's source sets and protected travel inputs, or marks
the case as requiring an isolated reach rebuild. Stress options with changed
sources cannot reuse a headline political map silently. Grain trade flows
themselves are rebuilt and audited in every seasonal case.
