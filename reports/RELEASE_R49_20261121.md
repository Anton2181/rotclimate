The atlas now runs the adopted Earth physics **r49** in full (all keys, not only the earlier `e3` subset), on a world geometry

## 1. Physics

r49 (reports/CLIMATE_R49_20261120.md): rain belt anchored by large plateaus (e85), suppressed convection ahead of the monsoon
onset (e86), 1,750 m plateau anchor (e87). Adopted on Earth under policy_v3: dev49 S 0.9875 vs r48, fine28 1.0038, unseen
sealed set n 0.978. Open: the North American and Sahel monsoon peaks remain weak (Mexico July–August about a quarter of
observed).
