# Earth r40 carried into the atlas, hosted copies brought up to date, 31 October 2026

**Rule.** A release that performs better on the Earth test updates the atlas
(user instruction, 31 October 2026). r40 is the adopted Earth profile
(31-region gate S 0.9932, 28 km 0.9953, sealed six 0.9671;
`reports/PHYSICS_GAPS_20261030.md` §12), so the repository Earth-fit atlas
now uses r40. The map-zone protections that rejected the r40 world refit
(`reports/R40_WORLD_20261031.md`) are reported below, not used as a gate.

## What the Earth-fit atlas now is

r40's shared physics on the published world's geometry: latitude,
orientation, elevation tiers and surroundings stay those fitted under r38, and
planetary size, spin, tilt, orbit and stellar input are unchanged. The climate
is the verified native checkpoint of the world refit's start candidate
(downsample 8, mean of surroundings layouts 1–3, all numerical checks
passed), exported through the normal pipeline (`scripts/build_atlas_variant.py`):
climate layers, farming, population lab, FORGE foraging, monthly winds,
political reach with its four gap fills, directed trade with three views,
vector files. The two Fit-check diagnostics the r38 atlas had were rebuilt
for r40 (`scripts/add_r40_atlas_diagnostics.py`):

* surroundings sensitivity: r40 on the same nine generated layouts at ds16;
  median spread 13.7 % of annual rain and 0.75 °C (r38: 14.0 %, 0.78 °C);
* initialization check: three extra spin-up years change monthly rain by at
  most 0.05 mm on mapped land; the annual cycles repeat.

| map measure | published r38 | r40 |

| seasonal zone-fit objective | 0.1828 | 0.1796 |
| exact painted-zone agreement | 35.0 % | 33.1 % |
| Whittaker objective (strict) | 0.3480 | 0.3466 |

These are fits to the painted zones of an invented world, a different test
from the Earth regions that adopted r40; r40 fits the painting slightly less
well, as r39 did. The r38 atlas is kept locally under
`output/atlas_r40/previous_atlases/earth_fit_r38/`.

## Earth-corrected topic (31 October 2026)

The two Earth correction stages trained on r40
(`calibration/earth_correction_r40_20261031`; held out by geographic cluster:
monthly temperature 1.807 → 1.548 °C, rain loss 0.4329 → 0.4201, 14 km
temperature 1.913 → 1.283 °C) are applied to the Earth-fit atlas's own
verified climate and shown as a new **Corrected** map topic
(`scripts/add_corrected_layers.py`). The corrections run on the model grid
(mean of the three surroundings layouts), the temperature change and rain
ratio are carried into the full-resolution fields like every other climate
layer, and eight layers are drawn: Köppen, Whittaker biomes and simulated
zones under the corrected climate, corrected year-mean temperature and
precipitation, and three maps of the change (year-mean temperature ±1 °C,
seasonal swing ±4 °C, yearly rain ±20 %). The station card and every other
topic, including farming, population, foraging and politics, keep the raw
simulation; the corrected layers are an alternative reading, not inputs.
The script refuses a correction trained on other shared physics.

| map measure (mean climate) | raw r40 | corrected |
|---|---:|---:|
| exact painted-zone agreement | 33.1 % | 35.6 % |
| mean zone membership | 0.281 | 0.293 |
| cold-wet / warm-wet / Mediterranean recall | 0.686 / 0.369 / 0.665 | 0.738 / 0.422 / 0.682 |
| tree / hot-dry recall | 0.070 / 0.231 | 0.110 / 0.249 |
| land changing Köppen class | — | 8.2 % |
| mean / mean absolute monthly temperature change | — | −0.25 / 0.86 °C |
| yearly rain on land | — | −1.2 % |

The change is mostly seasonal, as on Earth (maritime coasts a month late and
too continental): the year mean moves by −0.5 to +0.1 °C (5th–95th
percentile), the warmest-minus-coldest swing by −3.9 to +0.8 °C, strongest
in the east of the map; rain moves −12 % to +17 %, following the wind over
the terrain. The painted zones were not used to build the corrections, so the
higher agreement is an independent sign that they move the climate the right
way; it is still a fit to an invented world.

Checks: `scripts/check_atlas_smoke.js` now opens every Corrected layer and
requires its image to load; the local and packaged Earth fit pass with no
page errors or failed requests and twelve topics
(`output/atlas_r40/browser_corrected_{local,packed}.json`). Hosted: Earth fit
version 14, hub version 9 (it mentions the topic and the agreement change).
Figure: `output/atlas_r40/corrected_layers.png`.

```sh
python -I scripts/add_corrected_layers.py atlases/earth_fit --checkpoint output/r40_world/candidate_001.pkl --corrections calibration/earth_correction_r40_20261031
python -I scripts/refresh_atlas_pages.py && python -I scripts/build_fit_hub.py
```

## Farming, population and foraging on the Earth-corrected climate (31 October 2026)

The Earth-fit atlas can now recompute its food and people topics on the
corrected climate: the **Climate for food and people** control (shown on
Overview, Farming, Health and ecology, Population and Foraging layers) or
`#climate=corrected` in the address switches every farming, health,
population and FORGE layer, the station card's farming readouts, the
Population lab and the Foraging panel to packets built on the corrected
climate. Political reach, trade and the climate topics keep the simulated
climate.

`scripts/add_corrected_food.py` copies the climate packet, applies the
corrections to the only climate the three exporters read (monthly sea-level
temperature and rain in the packet's solstice bins, and the Whittaker biome
array), checks that the checkpoint reproduces the packet first, reruns the
unchanged farming, population and foraging exporters on the copy and
attaches their packets, images and layers under `data/corrected/`
(`corrected_food.json` records the inputs and totals).

| total | simulated | corrected |
|---|---:|---:|
| mixed food ceiling | 253.6 M | 257.5 M |
| central occupation scenario | 60.2 M | 61.2 M |
| frontier / settled-core scenarios | 25.8 / 151.8 M | 26.3 / 154.5 M |
| foraging scenario people | 1.307 M | 1.310 M |
| land changing biome | — | 7.2 % |

Locally the settlement food capacity moves −12 % to +25 % (5th–95th
percentile): the northern coasts and the eastern islands gain, the south
coast loses up to 40 % where the corrected climate is drier
(`output/atlas_r40/corrected_food_change.png`). The foraging change is
speckled cell to cell (±20 %): the finite-horizon FORGE scenario's
boom-and-bust cycles change phase with small changes of input, which the
robustness work on FORGE addresses next.

Checks: the browser smoke check now reloads the page in corrected mode and
requires the corrected farming totals, Population lab and Foraging totals to
load from `data/corrected/`; the local and packaged Earth fit pass, the
free and Whittaker fits are unaffected. The packager renames nested
packet binaries (`corrected_food.*`). Hosted Earth fit: version 15.
