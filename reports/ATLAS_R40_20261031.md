# Earth r40 carried into the atlas, hosted copies brought up to date, 31 October 2026

**Rule.** A release that performs better on the Earth test updates the atlas
(user instruction, 31 October 2026). r40 is the adopted Earth profile
(31-region gate S 0.9932, 28 km 0.9953, sealed six 0.9671;
`reports/PHYSICS_GAPS_20261030.md` §12), so the repository Earth-fit atlas
now uses r40. The map-zone protections that rejected the r40 world refit
(`reports/R40_WORLD_20261031.md`) are reported below, not used as a gate.

## What the Earth-fit atlas now is

r40's shared physics on the published world's geometry: latitude,
orientation, elevation tiers and surroundings stay those fitted under r38, and
planetary size, spin, tilt, orbit and stellar input are unchanged. The climate
is the verified native checkpoint of the world refit's start candidate
(downsample 8, mean of surroundings layouts 1–3, all numerical checks
passed), exported through the normal pipeline (`scripts/build_atlas_variant.py`):
climate layers, farming, population lab, FORGE foraging, monthly winds,
political reach with its four gap fills, directed trade with three views,
vector files. The two Fit-check diagnostics the r38 atlas had were rebuilt
for r40 (`scripts/add_r40_atlas_diagnostics.py`):

* surroundings sensitivity: r40 on the same nine generated layouts at ds16;
  median spread 13.7 % of annual rain and 0.75 °C (r38: 14.0 %, 0.78 °C);
* initialization check: three extra spin-up years change monthly rain by at
  most 0.05 mm on mapped land; the annual cycles repeat.

These are fits to the painted zones of an invented world, a different test
from the Earth regions that adopted r40; r40 fits the painting slightly less
well, as r39 did. The r38 atlas is kept locally under
`output/atlas_r40/previous_atlases/earth_fit_r38/`.

## Checks

* Packet, food budget, population and directed-trade checks pass
  (`scripts/build_r39_earth_stage.py` verifiers); the r40 packet has the same
  layers and metadata keys as the r38 one.
* Browser (`scripts/check_atlas_smoke.js`, Chromium): all three local atlases
  and their packaged copies load with no page errors or failed requests; every
  atlas has eleven topics; the Foraging panel loads its packet and totals in
  each, the Population and Land-and-people panels fill, the trade layer offers
  three views, and the Earth fit shows both sensitivity layers with their grid
  download and the initialization-check link.
* Pages are refreshed from the current template (`scripts/refresh_atlas_pages.py`);
  the hub (`scripts/build_fit_hub.py`) now describes the Earth fit as r40, lists
  eleven topics including Foraging and compares the three foraging scenarios.

The claude.ai artifacts were several builds behind (the Earth fit was an r37
build, and none had FORGE foraging, the political variant selector, the
directed-trade views, grid downloads or the Earth diagnostics). All four are
republished from the current local pages:

`scripts/package_atlas_artifact.py` now rewrites every packet's binary
reference when it renames `.bin` files to gzip-compressed `.wasm` (it handled
harvest and population only, so FORGE foraging would have failed to load in
the hosted copies) and leaves out the `.npz` model inputs no page reads.

Evidence: `output/atlas_r40/earth_publication.json`, `browser_*.json`,
`diagnostics/diagnostics.json`.

## Reproduce

```sh
python -I scripts/build_atlas_variant.py --params calibration/map_world_r40/finalists/candidate_001.json --name earth_fit_r40_stage --title "Rotclimate Earth-Fit Atlas" --eyebrow "Climate, farming and settlement · three layouts · c.200 AD" --note "…" --downsample 8 --image-scale 2 --seeds 1,2,3 --workers 2 --fit-objective zonefit --require-verified --checkpoint output/r40_world/candidate_001.pkl
python -I scripts/add_r40_atlas_diagnostics.py atlases/earth_fit_r40_stage --out output/atlas_r40/diagnostics
python -I scripts/refresh_atlas_pages.py && python -I scripts/build_fit_hub.py
python -I scripts/build_fit_hub.py --artifact output/atlas_r40/artifact_pack/hub
python -I scripts/package_atlas_artifact.py --atlas atlases/earth_fit --out output/atlas_r40/artifact_pack/earth_fit
node scripts/check_atlas_smoke.js http://127.0.0.1:8765/atlases/earth_fit/
```
