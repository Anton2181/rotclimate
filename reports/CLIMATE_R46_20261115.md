## What changed

New physics (opt-in switches added today, now on in r46):
* **e83 `trade_heat_low` 0.5**: the imposed trade winds give way where the model's own pressure falls poleward toward a heat low
  (Bengal's spring northerlies; trades belong to the subtropical high's equatorward flank only).
* **e77 `storm_convection` 0.51**: summer showers scaled by the storm index (troughs and fronts), warm and moist days only.
* **e78 `ice_saturation` 1.0**: below 0 °C the rain-maker judges the column against saturation over ice.
* e79 `edge_carry` 0.09 (from 0.19 in r45).

Refitted: convective rain 0.365 → 0.484, eddy rate 1.43 → 1.67, instability boost 2.52 → 1.87, rain time 9.3 → 9.7 days, RH
threshold 0.386 → 0.369, storm track 4.27 → 4.12, cold-high storm damping 0.31 → 0.40. The e75 rain-shadow barrier stays off.
