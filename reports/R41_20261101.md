# r41: the spring physics, adopted (1 November 2026)

**The first physics adoption since r40.** r41 = r40 + four mechanisms built
in this round (all opt-in switches, set by a frozen refit):

| switch | r41 value | what it does |
|---|---:|---|
| e65 `ebm_land_ice` | 0.466 | the latitude-mean land column keeps only 47 % of its temperature-driven snow albedo (ice sheets keep all): maritime land no longer inherits the average continent's snowy spring |
| e68 `storm_baroclinic` (+ `storm_baroclinic_cold_c` 10 °C) | 0.252 | the storm band moves a quarter of the way toward each meridian's own baroclinic zone, counted where the air is cool |
| e69 `wet_sun` | 0.439 | sunshine-driven wet-ground cooling (Bowen ratio by soil wetness and insolation), beside a weaker annual-mean cooling (`wet_cooling` 0.50 -> 0.43) |
| e31 `continental_mean` | 7.31 -> 6.29 | the cold-air pool, rebalanced |
| also refitted | | `continentality` 0.56 -> 0.44, `snow_albedo` 1.34 -> 0.21, `land_offset` -0.68 -> -0.98 |

Parameters: `calibration/local_accuracy_20261023/r41_params.json` (=
`calibration/spring_refit_20261101/candidate41_params.json`).

## How it was chosen

Plan `calibration/spring_refit_20261101/plan.json`, frozen before any
result: CMA-ES over eight knobs from the land-ice refit's best candidate,
scored as the gate scores against r40 with the retired sealed six as an extra
guarded group. The search stopped at 60 candidates. Its lowest-cost
candidate (#59, S 0.9819) failed policy v2 on the training group (1.001) in
the frozen validation, with sealed set b not run
(`output/wet_sun_20261101/validate_59.log`). A frozen amendment
(`calibration/spring_refit_20261101/amendment_candidate41.json`, written with
the dev31 numbers known and sealed set b unseen) then took the
lowest-cost candidate that passed every development check in the search,
#41, through the same validation.

## What it does and does not fix

The win is in the mid-latitudes (group 0.975: Pacific NW, eastern Europe,
Manchuria, western Europe) and the cold continental interiors (Mongolia),
where the band's snowy spring and the planet-wide storm band were wrong.
East Asia's subtropical spring warmth is reduced only a little. The hot
deserts and the monsoon plateau, already too cold, get slightly colder: annual
bias Sahara −2.55 → −2.64 °C, Sahel −3.00 → −3.11, Najd −4.82 → −4.96 and
Deccan −2.99 → −3.02, with seasonal swings 4.5–5.5 °C too small in the
Sahara and Najd. (Corrected 1 November; an earlier version of this paragraph
said "warmer-wrong".) Together with the maritime lag
(reports/SEASON_PHASE_20261031.md), these are the next physics targets.

## In the Earth-fit atlas (released 1 November)

`output/atlas_r41/release.sh`: the atlas rebuilt on r41 physics with the
published world's geometry (layouts 1-3, ds8, verified numerics), the r40
atlas kept at `output/atlas_r41/previous_atlases/earth_fit_r40`; the Earth
corrections retrained on r41 (`calibration/earth_correction_r41_20261101`;
held out by cluster: monthly temperature 1.761 → 1.572 °C, −11 %; at 14 km
1.850 → 1.400 °C; rain loss 0.4327 → 0.4193, −3 %; sealed set b, not a
training region, 2.42 → 2.06 °C); the corrected climate re-exported as the
atlas's default with the raw simulation one switch away, and the three
correction-change maps under Fit checks. Browser checks pass locally and as
packaged (`output/atlas_r41/browser_packaged*.json`); the hosted hub and the
three atlases are republished.

34.9 % strict agreement. The world refit with planet levers
(`calibration/map_world_r41_planet`) is the attempt to win the painted zones
back without touching the Earth-calibrated physics.
