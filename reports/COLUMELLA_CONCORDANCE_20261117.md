# Six calendar expressions checked against a printed witness

All six selected paragraphs agree exactly between the Latin Library and the
Wikisource digital transcription. The linked printed volume, however, has a
substantive variant in one selected expression:11.2.46 lacks the digital word
`bis`. This result preserves a source difference; it does not choose a new
ploughing rate or silently correct the first calendar packet.

## Edition and provenance

The [Wikisource page](https://la.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=De_Re_Rustica_(Gesner)/Liber_XI&oldid=134877)
links [Columellae opera.djvu](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Columellae_opera.djvu).
Its scanned titlepage5 reads *Scriptores rei rusticae veteres Latini*,
*e recensione Jo.Matth.Gesneri*,volume2 containing Columella,with the Biponti
Societatis imprint and1787 date. The titlepage and six selected text-page
images were inspected; OCR supplies a separately retained literal check.

The Wikisource index labels the scan **Pseudo-Gesner/Pseudo-1787**, while
Commons/Internet Archive metadata describe the1787 volume. Those labels are
retained without an invented explanation. The printed imprint is verified as
printed; a critically established edition genealogy is not. The Latin Library
witness's exact edition likewise remains unverified.

Identical digital paragraphs do not establish independent textual descent or
prove that a transcription reproduces every detail of its linked scan. The
comparison therefore distinguishes the two digital pages from the printed
evidence. These are witnesses of the same primary chapter,not independent
historical calibration cohorts.

## The six bounded comparisons

| Columella11.2 | Printed page / scan page | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 422 / 428 | Half-month/fifteen-day expression agrees |
| 6 | 423 / 429 | The unusual `semestrium` term is also printed;duration remains unassigned |
| 9 | 424 / 430 | Printed `quatuor fibrarum`,digital `quattuor fibrarum`;orthography retained |
| 26 | 428 / 434 | Three modii per opera wording agrees;`sarit/sarrit` spelling differs |
| 46 | 433 / 439 | Printed line omits `bis`;digital texts include it |
| 94 | 445 / 451 | Both source solstice identifiers agree in numeral/spelled-number form |

The printed line in46 reads `lirantur autem jugera duo opera una`;the digital
target is `lirantur autem iugera duo bis opera una`. Long-s,linebreak and
`i/j` spelling are distinguished from the presence/absence of a whole word.
The scan supports the recorded omission directly; it is not an OCR-only claim.
No reading is selected for a model coefficient,and the older packet remains
byte-identical with its unresolved clause.

The source markers in94 remain `XVI Kal.Ian.` / `VIIII Kal.Ian.`,with the
printed spelled forms and their Hipparchus/Chaldean attributions. Recognizing
the same Roman identifier across typography is not a Gregorian/model-bin
conversion. `Fibrae`,seed-volume/area equivalence,`opera` duration and full
occupation timing remain unqualified.
