# r43: a small calibration gain, with effectively tied unseen performance

R43 is the prospectively selected start-1, evaluation-7 candidate from all
39 evaluations in the completed three-start campaign. It changes only the nine
declared r42 strengths/gates, wet-sun response and precipitation timescale.
The planet/orbit and imposed forcing choices are unchanged; winds and SST are
model-generated. It introduces no regional correction coefficients or new
physical closure. Exact parameters and the adoption receipt are in
`calibration/local_accuracy_20261023/r43_params.json` and `r43_adoption.json`.

It is adopted under the unchanged policy v3:

| Check | Combined error / r42 | Temperature error | Rain loss | Result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Fresh paired dev49 | 0.9913780 | -0.2658% | -1.4586% | pass |
| Paired 28 km, four declared regions | 1.0080705 | +1.7873% | -0.1732% | pass |
| Previously unseen set e, six regions | 0.9997909 | +1.3325% | -1.3744% | pass |

The fine-grid gate allows at most 1% worsening of the combined score. The
unseen gate requires the combined score not to worsen. Those rules were fixed
before results and were not replaced by individual-region or group vetoes.
**The unseen combined score is effectively tied**, not evidence of a large
generalization improvement. The fine-grid and unseen temperature regressions
are retained. This is a small calibration step, not a physical breakthrough.

Both full 49-region error vectors reproduce the search values to 1e-10 and
the reconstructed reference's actual forcing footprints match. The finalist,
all search records and source hashes were committed before fresh validation;
development/fine qualification was committed before any sealed-e scoring.
Every case and raw monthly packet is retained in
`output/r42_multistart_validation_20261102`. The validation driver did not adopt
the candidate; the separate adoption receipt records the reviewed decision.

The wider context, unequal regional tradeoffs and limits of the three-start
search are in `reports/MULTISTART_20261103.md`. The independent conservative
transport experiments use their declared r42 references and remain separate;
none of their failed variants is adopted by this release.

Set e is now spent and must not be reused as unseen validation. Set f was
reserved before any e outcome; its six scored boxes remain unintegrated and
unscored. Future development pools should be declared prospectively, including
spent regions where appropriate, without silently changing the current S49.

## Atlas publication

The r43 world parameters copy the published r42 geometry and every non-shared
setting unchanged, replacing only the shared physical parameters. They are
stored in `calibration/map_world_r43/params.json`.

The served Earth atlas remains r42 until the full r43 staging run, new matching
correction training, climate/food/population/foraging/trade exports, language
restoration and browser/checkout/portable checks finish. This is intentional
atomic publication: newer calibration code does not relabel older map data.
The launcher identifies the actual served snapshot. Free-fit and Whittaker
map data are unchanged.
