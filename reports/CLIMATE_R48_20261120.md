## Physics: e86

Over land, the warm-moist convective boost acts only equatorward of the monsoon belt. It ramps off between (ITCZ − gap) and 5°
beyond: dry air aloft overrides the shallow moist layer, as over the Sahel before the July onset. The refit sets k = 1.0 and
gap = 2.6°. The storm-driven boost (e77) is untouched.
