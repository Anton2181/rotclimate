## A premise corrected

The plan said r41 made the hot deserts warmer, and blamed e69's warming of
dry ground. **That is wrong.**

r41's deserts are too **cold**:

| region | annual bias | seasonal-range error |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sahara | −2.6 °C | −5.5 °C |
| Sahel | −3.1 °C | −1.6 °C |
| Najd | −5.0 °C | −4.5 °C |
| Deccan | −3.0 °C | −1.2 °C |

r41 made them slightly colder than r40 did. The cause is r41's lower land
offset (−0.98 against −0.44), which more than offsets e69's warming of dry
ground. The search did try `wet_sun_ref` near 0.6, which warms dry ground,
but those candidates cost the training group.

The error the frozen plan should have targeted: dry land's summer is too
mild. That is the next physics design. Its development evidence is the
retired desert set, now development data.
