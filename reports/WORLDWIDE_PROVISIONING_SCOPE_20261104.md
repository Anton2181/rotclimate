# Provisioning scope: all societies — 4 November 2026

User direction: include the other polities, despite their sparse spreadsheet
evidence, and communities outside mapped polities, including mobile pastoral
and other tribal societies. Imperial commanderies are one documented case,
not the template or sole target for the world model.

## Shared foundation

Build production, consumption, seasonal storage, water access and exchange
across inhabited/suitable land independently of painted claims. Keep political
organization, livelihood, mobility and settlement form as separate dimensions.
Pastoralism does not by itself imply mobility or absence of a polity; a tribal
community need not be mobile or exclusively pastoral. Mixed strategies must
remain possible. Environmental constraints do not determine institutional
sophistication or form a universal ranking of societal development.

Conserve population, land, feed, food and labour within a specified scenario.
Existing farming/pastoral and FORGE scenarios cannot simply be added as if
they occupied independent land or supported additional independent populations.
Shared resource use needs explicit accounting. Seasonal pasture/water limits,
herd maintenance and reproduction, and travel costs must precede strong claims
about sustainable mobile populations. The present annual grazing proxy is not
a seasonal herd or pastoral-mobility simulation.

## Institutions and evidence

Apply documented capital/province/commandery links only where evidence supports
them. For other societies, retain known settlements and claims while labelling
unknown centres, membership and organization as unknown. Explore declared
alternatives rather than silently supplying an imperial hierarchy or deriving
administrative rank from an icon or biome. Richer documentation must not give
the Empire a built-in advantage in the general model.

Local food availability is distinct from resources that an institution can
collect, exchange or redistribute. Tax, tribute, reciprocal exchange, market
purchase and household sharing are different supplied institutional scenarios.
Provisioning can describe households, villages, camps and gatherings as well
as towns and garrisons. An unrecorded tax system does not imply zero social
organization or an automatically available state surplus.

## Implementation implications

The current proposed-town generator is restricted to painted claims
(`polity/fields.py`, `polity/settle.py`) and substantially represents
administrative/market seats. That restriction must not define where people
can exist. Simply expanding that same seat-count generator to all land would
also be inappropriate. Introduce village/camp and livelihood-specific scenarios
with conserved population/resources, while retaining the old administrative
proposal as a separate labelled baseline. Unclaimed land is not empty land.

The next provisioning pilot should cover contrasting societies: an agricultural
polity outside the Empire, a mobile pastoral/mixed-livelihood case, and a settled
community beyond mapped state claims. Select and document geographic inputs
before tuning results. Sparse institutional evidence remains explicit; these
are scenario comparisons, not invented ethnographic observations.

Prioritize broadly applicable maps: seasonal food/feed deficits, dry-season
pasture and water constraints, stores required to bridge scarcity, potential
camp/settlement locations, and exchange dependence. Add institutional collection
and military-support overlays as separately specified cases. Keep all normal
atlases and their raw/corrected packets matched. This scope note adopts no new
coefficients and changes no published population, climate or political map.

The earlier research foundations still apply: FORGE for terrestrial foraging
mechanisms, explicit uncertainty in settlement/network models, and the
distinction between production and appropriability. Pastoral herd dynamics,
mobility and exchange parameters require their own academic-method review and
testing; Roman military ration assumptions must not become universal household
or pastoral coefficients. See the existing
[control/settlement research](CONTROL_SETTLEMENT_PROPOSALS_20261104.md) and
[administrative hierarchy limits](ADMINISTRATIVE_HIERARCHY_20261104.md).
