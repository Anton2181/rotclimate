# Release r58 atlas (27 Nov 2026)

Owner choice: "Atlas rebuild on r58": put the gains since r51 into the map: r58 climate, the zonefit_rivers world
fit and drawn rivers shown in full. Driver `scripts/run_r58_atlas.sh`, chain `scripts/release_r58.sh` (as r51).

## Physics on the world: r58w
r58 (calibration/local_accuracy_20261023/r58_adoption.json; dev6 S97 0.971 vs r57, fine28 0.997, sealed Z 0.931) takes
land-edge column water from NCEP observations at each point (e116), which an invented world does not have. The atlas
runs **r58w** = r58 with `edge_pw_source: "zonal"` (e116z): the globe's land-mean NCEP column water at the cell's
latitude and month. Earth screen against r58 (97 regions, >1 worse): zonal **1.0098** [1.0012, 1.0187]; edge off
1.0265 [1.0106, 1.0449] (calibration/map_world_r58_planet/edge_forcing.md). Earth physics stays r58.

## World geometry (W6)
Guarded search (calibrate mode `guarded_rivers` = zonefit + 0.2 x drawn-river width ranking; guards = the start
world's own coarse control), 241 evaluations at ds32 over layouts 1, 2, 3, 17, 23: no candidate beat the start
(0.263). Selected: the r51 world geometry with r58w physics (calibration/map_world_r58/selection.json).

follows the latest Earth physics regardless of zone agreement.

## Other changes in the chain
- Rivers: drawn rivers always shown in full (routing.segments `always=`), discharge by Priority-Flood on height map v2.2.
- Presence and mobility: the seasonal-grassland pilot's home has no resolved forage under r58w, so it is recorded
  `home_not_traversable` (unscored) at the local and regional scales; builder and review now carry unscored pilots.

dry interiors (Kalahari, inland Australia) too dry.
