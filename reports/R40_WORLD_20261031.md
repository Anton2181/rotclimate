# The invented world on r40 physics (no publication), 31 October 2026

**Result.** The fantasy map keeps the published r38 world. A frozen refit of
the world's geometry on r40's shared physics
(`calibration/map_world_r40/plan.json`, `scripts/world_campaign_r40.py`)
finds coarse candidates that beat the old world's zone objective, but every
one of them loses a protected climate zone or the Mediterranean guard. The
only candidate that clears the coarse screen, the starting geometry itself,
fails every native-resolution protection. The nine audit layouts were not
run and stay unseen.

## Plan

r40's shared physics with the published r38 world's geometry as the start
(planet, orbit, tilt, retrograde spin and map scale locked); CMA-ES over
latitude, orientation, the four elevation tiers and the surroundings at
downsample 32, 97 evaluations, training on the mean climate of the five
known layouts (1, 2, 3, 17, 23: 17 and 23 became development data in the r39
campaigns). The coarse screen, native ds8 check and final nine-layout audit
with a paired bootstrap follow the r39 rules unchanged
(`reports/R39_WORLD_TRANSFER_20261026.md`).

## What it means

r40's gains are measured on Earth's 31 regions against observations. The
invented world is scored differently: painted climate zones on a retrograde
planet with generated surroundings. There r40's physics makes the painted
Mediterranean drier (dry share 6.4 → 9.0 % at native resolution) and loses
both wet zones' recall, and refitting the geometry only moves the losses
between zones. The same happened to r39 (four rejected attempts). The atlas
keeps r38's verified world; the r40 Earth corrections
(`calibration/earth_correction_r40_20261031`) apply to r40 Earth runs, not
to the published world.

The coarse search ran while e61 (an opt-in switch, off) was added to the
model; r40's Earth cells reproduce bit for bit with the new code, and the
native run used it.
