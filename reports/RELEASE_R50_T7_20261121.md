# Earth-fit atlas, r50 + T7 rules — full rebuild (21 November 2026)

Task T7 of the autonomous plan (HANDOFF.md). Same r50 climate, world and Earth corrections as
reports/RELEASE_R50_20261121.md; three adopted rules wired in and the whole chain rebuilt (scripts/release_r50_t7.sh).

## 1. What changed

* **Runoff amount: Budyko–Fu** (reports/RUNOFF_AMOUNT_20261121.md; rotclimate/routing.py, w = 2.6) sizes river discharge.
* **Grazing seasons: GPP-tested forage phenology** (reports/FORAGE_SEASON_20261121.md; harvest/pasture.py rain memory,
  data/pasture_model.json: temperature span 15 → 20 °C, rain half-saturation 50 → 100 mm).
* **Town sizes: r5 fit merged into the main chain** (calibration/settlement_r5_merge_20261121; spec
  calibration/settlement_chain/release4_r50t7.json). Size model M1C coefficients [1.915, 0.147], c 0.8, σ 1.048 →
  [1.555, 0.183], c 1.0, σ 1.036. Town sites and counts are unchanged.

* The Fu runoff barely moves the atlas: irrigation draws on rivers in a few dozen hexes and food capacity is unchanged.
* The longer, rain-limited grazing season pushes about 2.4 points more grazing land into the needs-stored-fodder class,
  mostly along the southern coast and the eastern lowlands (map: output/rebuild_r50_t7/maps).
* The r5 size fit gives a slightly heavier middle of the size distribution (closer to Earth at the median) and a
  smaller corrected-climate top. On the raw climate, the imperial capitals still take the largest drawn areas (plan A2);
  Aēnaha and Kevi grow, Maēya and Haruva shrink.
* The release-3 control layer (settled_control_v21_*) is byte-identical to r50; the release-4 control (staffed by
  release-4 towns) loses about 1.2 points of held land as town sizes shift.
