# Existing work evidence and scheduler reuse — 17 November 2026

The existing schedulers already enforce operation windows, simultaneous resource
requirements and one shared staff budget. They do not provide historical task
timing or a calibrated warehouse-handling intensity. This read-only audit
separates22 exact source/interface statements and10 archived task counts into
prescribed, assumed, model-derived, implemented and missing-input categories.
It runs no solver and adopts no coefficient.

## What is actually sourced

The archived Columella2.12 transcription prescribes wheat10.5 and barley6.5
workdays per iugerum to the threshing floor. It contains six wheat/four barley
listed task entries. The totals sum exactly. It is estate advice, not a measured
complete occupation or a universal work function. The eight-hour day and
0.25ha/iugerum conversion are separate assumptions, yielding the old336/208
listed hours per hectare. They are not newly qualified ancient observations.

The archived farm demo's six operation windows are explicitly manufactured.
Its land/output and time/resource accounting can be reused, but those windows
cannot supply the missing Earth monthly baseline. Existing harvest-boundary
masks are model calendar alternatives, not independently validated dates.

The old monthly collection study also states its load and handling assumptions:
60,000/120,000 kcal per trip,0.25 handling hours and a600h annual staff envelope
split into50h bins. The same food-supported staff are shared across duties.
Those are stress scenarios, not observed porter capacities, warehouse rates or
historical staffing. Original collector round-trip days cannot be inferred
from the warehouse network's road-km costs.

The LP has no automatic task precedence, travel, hiring or food storage. The
monthly staff gate refuses an unchanged plan when work fails; it does not cut
shipments and keep claiming the original residents remain fed. These limits
must survive any future integration. Neither unused time nor a successful
manufactured check supplies a new workforce.
