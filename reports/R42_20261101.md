# r42: the desert physics, adopted on overall accuracy (1 November 2026)

**r42 = r41 + the descending-branch physics.** It adds:
- **e71:** compression warming of sinking air in the Hadley cell's
  descending branch, gated against rain, plateaus and continental air;
- **e70b:** drying of that branch's core, switched off under raining air
  (e70c).

`wet_sun` and `precip_tau_days` were also refitted. Parameters:
`calibration/local_accuracy_20261023/r42_params.json`, which is candidate 1
of desert refit #3 (`calibration/desert_refit3_20261101`; the search is
described in `reports/DESERT_HEAT_20261101.md`).

## The rule it was adopted under: policy v3

`calibration/local_accuracy_20261023/policy_v3.json`. On 1 November the
map's owner set aside the rule that no group of regions may get worse:
"I want the overall accuracy to keep getting better."

**Policy v3 adopts a candidate when:**
- its score over all 49 development regions (dev31 plus the retired
  sealed sets a, b and c) improves by at least 0.5 %;
- neither temperature nor rain gets more than 1 % worse;
- the paired 28 km check is within 1 %;
- the next unseen sealed set, run once, is not worse.

Group scores are reported but no longer gate.

**The order of events matters here:**
- Under v2, desert refits #2 and #3 had both been rejected on group
  scores alone. Refit #3's best overall candidate then worsened the
  mid-latitude group by only 0.2 %.
- The candidate was chosen under v3 from the two searches' 160 completed
  candidates before anything else was run
  (`calibration/desert_refit3_20261101/v3_selection.json`). The rule: the
  lowest 49-region score with temperature and rain within 1 % on both the
  31 and the 49 regions.
- Sealed set d, declared on 1 November and never run before, was the
  deciding test.

- **Better:** the deserts and the dry belt. Set b's error falls by 15 %,

## 2 November: reference reconstruction

A fresh-container r42 control reproduced 48 of the 49 development-region
error pairs exactly. Tripolitania differed: its declared geography rebuilt
with 182 scored land cells, while the archived run used 195. All original
scientific-source and WorldClim archive hashes still match. The other 48
actual case hashes reproduce. The old Tripolitania aggregate cache is not
available, so its precise cause remains unresolved; the historical cache
key omits geographic coordinates and source identities.

Fresh paired r41/r42 tests on all six retired-c regions passed the strict
numerical checks, with raw per-cell packets retained in
`output/r42_reference_recovery_20261102`. Substituting this matched pair into
the 49-region comparison gives **S49 0.9628116258** (temperature −4.62%, rain
−2.82%), versus the original archived 0.9626111459. The conclusion that r42
improves overall error is unchanged. The original evidence above is retained
rather than rewritten. This does not rerun or retune on sealed set d.

The next search uses the reconstructed references under frozen revision
`calibration/r42_multistart_20261102b`; the original pilot stopped before any
non-control candidate. The current completed control matches every recovered
reference error pair to 1e-10. Recovery receipts include the full case audit,
paired summaries, raw fields and source identities. Sealed set e is untouched.
