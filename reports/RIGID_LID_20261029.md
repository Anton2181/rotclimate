# Retaining mean circulation — 29 October 2026

The pressure prototype can now retain a divergence-free vertically averaged
wind while conserving column dry mass and accounting for gas/kinetic/rain energy.
All twelve precision-refined Earth-input repeats completed. The new capability
does **not** cure the initial rainfall artifact or establish improved climate
accuracy: India and Mexico still put essentially all rain at the perimeter,
and the Pacific Northwest puts 99.8% there in the retained-mean finer run.

## Physics and paired inputs

The old prototype imposed zero depth-mean wind at every face. Fixed pressure lids
only require zero column divergence. The extension allows nonzero mean circulation
and diagnoses a pressure multiplier enforcing that less restrictive condition.
In dry-mass-weighted face velocities, `D x = 0`, and the constraint gradient is
`G = −Dᵀ`. The Crank–Nicolson momentum step solves the saddle system

`[I + dt A/2, dt G; D, 0] [x_new, pressure] = [momentum_rhs, 0]`.

One redundant mass equation is dropped to fix the pressure gauge. Constraint
pressure does zero work on an admissible midpoint velocity. The hydrostatic
thermal pressure and compression remain paired as before. Nonlinear momentum
advection and evolving surface pressure are still absent; this is a rigid-lid
approximation, not a complete primitive-equation model.

Both archived **simulated** lower and upper winds are staggered onto the same
pressure grid. The lower source fills the first 25% dry mass; the upper source
fills the remaining five cells. Projected-up becomes grid-south with a sign flip.
The paired controls share identical baroclinic shear. One removes the entire
vertical mean; the other retains its mass-orthogonal divergence-free projection.
The initial projection's removed kinetic energy is recorded separately, without
hidden heat compensation or a claim of energy equivalence with the old archive.

Temperature, water, Earth rotation, five-day drag, one-hour convective relaxation,
224 km sampling and prescribed normal boundary flow remain fixed. The boundary
mean is still zero: the retained mean circulation is closed at the perimeter.
Incoming T/q remain fixed at adjacent initial model values. No observed wind,
humidity or rainfall is supplied, and there is no sustained solar/surface
evaporation forcing. Each case integrates six hours at 900 and 450 s.

## Next boundary limitation

This test retains internal mean circulation but still blocks mean flow across
the regional boundary. A subsequent conditional boundary should permit a declared
globally mass-balanced mean throughflow. Its constraint becomes `D x = −d_boundary`;
constraint pressure then exchanges work with the exterior, which must be included
as a boundary geopotential flux. Simply dropping the old zero-mean check would
create a dry-mass/energy error. This extension is not implemented or adopted in
these results. Thermal balance, terrain and sustained forcing remain unresolved.
