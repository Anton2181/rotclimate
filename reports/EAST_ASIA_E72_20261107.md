## The idea

**What happens on Earth:**
- **Cold season.** From autumn to late spring, the westerly jet runs along the southern flank of the Tibetan Plateau. East of it, it forms the Japan jet near 32–35° N.
- **Early summer.** The jet jumps north of the plateau. Until July, the Meiyu–Baiu front holds at 30–35° N.

**What the model does instead:**
- **A zonal band.** Its storms run in one band round the planet, bent by e68 toward each meridian's sharpest temperature contrast (r43: 25 %, among cool air only).
- **Too far north.** Over East China that band sits at 40–53° N all year. This is the error behind P6: storms over Manchuria and Korea, wet winters there, and dry, sunny, too-warm springs further south (`EAST_ASIA_P6_20261101.md`).

**The rule (e72).** It is general, so it would work on any map:
- **Where it acts:** downwind of a high plateau, meaning land above 2,500 m smoothed over ~150 km.
- **What it does:** it pulls the storm band toward the plateau's equatorward edge + 3°.
- **How strongly:** scaled by how much plateau lies upwind, as 1 − exp(−area / 1.5 M km²). Its influence fades downwind with a 3,000 km e-folding.
- **Direction:** retrograde spin reverses the westerlies.
- **Default:** off, and r43 reproduces bit for bit.

**Why each version led to the next:**
- **e72 never fired.** Over East China the model's band sits at 40–53° N all year, always poleward of Tibet's northern edge (37.8° N). The "band runs into the plateau" trigger never held.
- **e72b fired, but only where the window holds Tibet.** Each region runs in its own window with an 1,100 km pad. Tibet lies inside East China's window, so East China improved (×0.904), but Japan, Korea and Manchuria cannot see it.
- **The Andes point the same way.** South Brazil, downwind of the Altiplano, improved as much (×0.880). Since the rule was never tuned on the Andes, this is an independent sign that it is right.
- **e72c gives Earth regions their upwind plateau from the global elevation,** as the west fetch already does for upwind land (`earth.plateau_profile`, cached).

## On your world

The Earth-fit world has no high plateau: its smoothed terrain peaks near 1,070 m, against the 2,500 m threshold. So e72 changes nothing on the map itself. Its value for the atlas is indirect:
- the shared physics stops compensating for a missing East Asian mechanism;
- the Earth-trained corrections and future world refits inherit a better-calibrated model.
