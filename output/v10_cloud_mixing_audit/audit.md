Opportunity differences follow the realized state sequence; they can recount vapor and are not the rainfall of a different climate. They do not prove heating is unimportant after climate feedbacks.

A constructed 70%-RH parcel at 20°C rising from 1000 to 800hPa condenses. With a 20% parcel fraction, separately adjusted condensate is 0.451 g/kg mixture. Mixing first with dry environmental air gives 41.9% bulk RH and zero condensate. This demonstrates sensitivity to unresolved variability, not regional skill.

The 24 independent Jan-1 regional steps reproduce the bulk operator exactly and preserve water/energy under the parcel bound. Their increased per-probe rainfall cannot be extrapolated to annual totals.

Primary context: [ECMWF cloud-cover lecture](https://www.ecmwf.int/sites/default/files/elibrary/2005/16958-parametrization-cloud-cover.pdf) explains why subgrid humidity/temperature variability can form cloud below grid-average saturation. It does not validate this prototype or immediate rain fallout.
