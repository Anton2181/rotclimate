EARTH EVIDENCE · 14 NOVEMBER 2026

River water now reaches finite ponds and daily soil stores

The daily coupling is implemented and all 256 declared development cases are retained. These are water-budget scenarios with unqualified field parameters and forcing, not a calibrated harvest or settlement update.

One paid chain from river to field

River withdrawals are limited once at each node and rationed among fields by useful request. Delivery losses, finite infiltration, pond storage, pond evaporation, drainage, surface drying, deeper bare-soil diffusion and crop root uptake share the same water ledger. Drained water leaves this model; it is not credited again downstream.

The complete bare-soil FAO example now pays its deeper background evaporation separately, with zero crop uptake. All terrain candidates retain their water demand even when the prescribed crop is thermally ineligible.

All declared cases and matched controls

Two supplied calendars, two root depths, two bare coefficients, two pond capacities and two efficiencies are retained for both crops and regions, each with a rain-only control. The spread below describes the scenario grid, not statistical uncertainty.
