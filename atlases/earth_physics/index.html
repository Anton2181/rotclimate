        Models, assumptions and Earth reference

        Expected density = (crop food × biome clearing factor + grazing) × water access × health × occupation. Settlement potential = 100 × usable food / (usable food + 100 people/km²) × water access × health.

        Crop yields retain the Harvest period assumptions and Han calibration. The malaria thermal curve follows Mordecai et al. (2013); rainfall and irrigation add a declared moisture gate. Suitability is not infection prevalence. The maximum 35% population penalty is an assumption, removable above.

        Frontier, central and settled-core occupation use historical population / food-capacity analogue quantiles. They are not confidence intervals. HYDE and GLUES inform the distinction between resource potential and realized population; this composite does not reproduce either model.

        Cities, trade, fishing, soils, floods, wars and epidemics are not explicitly simulated. Grazing and biome clearing coefficients remain uncertain scenario choices.

        The local year

        Ten months of 35 days plus 15 Holy Days. With the winter solstice on Titian Marigold 23, every month's season name matches the sun.

        Each frame is one five-day week. Arrows are surface winds.

        Around the map

        Physics: a planet-wide seasonal energy balance (tilt and orbit), a 2-D model of winds, sea temperatures, air masses, moisture, rain and snow, and Köppen–Geiger classification. Unknown surroundings are procedurally generated; their land fraction per side is calibrated.
